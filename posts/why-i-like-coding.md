# Why I Like Coding

**Author:** Ed Donor  
**Date:** February 7, 2026

Coding is not just a skill; it's a passion. It allows me to create, innovate, and solve problems. The joy of seeing a program come to life and perform tasks as intended is unparalleled. Here are a few reasons why I love coding:

1. **Creativity**: Coding is like art. It gives me the freedom to build anything I can imagine.
2. **Problem-Solving**: Every bug is a puzzle waiting to be solved, and every solution is a victory.
3. **Continuous Learning**: The tech world evolves rapidly, and coding keeps me on my toes, always learning something new.

In conclusion, coding is more than just a profession for me; it's a way of life.
